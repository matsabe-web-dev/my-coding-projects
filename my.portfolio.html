My Journey

My journey into web development began with a curiosity about how websites are built and a desire to create meaningful digital experiences. Over time, I've developed a strong foundation in HTML and CSS, and I'm now expanding my skills to include JavaScript and modern development practices.

  1. Discovered web development and decided to learn to code.
  2. Started learning HTML and CSS through online tutorials and courses on freeCodeCamp, Codecademy, and Udemy.
  3. Built several small projects to practice my skills, including personal websites and simple web applications. Part of the projects I built was a fully accessible event schedule page for a fictional tech summit, featuring tables, forms, and embedded maps
  4. Explored JavaScript fundamentals and began integrating interactivity into my projects.
  5. Focused on accessibility and responsive design to ensure my websites are usable by everyone.
  6. Currently working on more complex projects that combine my coding skills with my entrepreneurial interests in real estate and AI-driven solutions. Planning and building Dilse & Co Realty, my upcoming residential real estate firm.
  7. Next up - learning CSS to bring these projects to life visually.

Frequently Asked Questions

What inspired you to become a web developer?

My inspiration came from a desire to create meaningful digital experiences and solve real-world problems through technology. I was fascinated by how websites are built and wanted to learn the skills necessary to bring my ideas to life.

What are your long-term career goals?

My long-term goal is to become a full-stack developer, combining my technical skills with my entrepreneurial interests in real estate and AI-driven solutions. I aim to create innovative products that make a positive impact on people's lives.

How do you stay updated with the latest web development trends?

I stay updated by following industry blogs, participating in online communities, attending webinars, and taking courses on platforms like freeCodeCamp, Codecademy, and Udemy.

What advice would you give to someone starting their journey in web development?

Start with the basics, focus on building a strong foundation in HTML and CSS, and practice by creating small projects. Don't be afraid to ask questions and seek help from the community. Consistency and persistence are key.

What are your favorite tools and resources for learning web development?

Some of my favorite tools and resources include freeCodeCamp, Codecademy, Udemy, MDN Web Docs, and Stack Overflow. I also enjoy following web development blogs and YouTube channels for tutorials and tips.

How do you approach problem-solving in your projects?

I approach problem-solving by breaking down complex issues into smaller, manageable tasks. I research solutions, experiment with different approaches, and seek feedback from peers or online communities when needed.

What are your thoughts on the future of web development?

I believe the future of web development will continue to evolve with advancements in AI, machine learning, and other emerging technologies. Developers will need to adapt to new tools and frameworks while maintaining a focus on accessibility, performance, and user experience.
