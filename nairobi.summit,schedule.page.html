About the Summit

The Nairobi Tech & Innovation Summit is East Africa's premier gathering for developers, digital marketers, entrepreneurs, and industry innovators. Now in its fifth year, the summit has grown from a single-day meetup of 1000 attendees into a two-day flagship event drawing over 5000 participants from Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and beyond.

Across two days, attendees can expect hands-on workshops covering modern web and mobile development, in-depth panel discussions on fintech and digital policy, and practical sessions on growing a brand through digital marketing in African markets. Beyond the talks, the summit is built around connection — dedicated networking breaks, a startup pitch competition, and an exhibition floor where local tech companies showcase their products to investors and the public.

Whether you're a student writing your first lines of code, a startup founder scaling your platform, or a marketer building a brand for the next generation of African consumers, the summit is designed to give you tools, connections, and inspiration to take back to your own work.

Venue Location

The summit will be held at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC), located in the heart of Nairobi, Kenya. The venue is easily accessible by public transport and offers ample parking for attendees.

Location: Kenyatta International Convention Centre, Harambee Avenue, Nairobi, Kenya.

For more information about the venue, including directions and nearby accommodations, please visit the KICC official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for the summit?

You can register for the summit by visiting our website and filling out the registration form. Early bird pricing ends September 30, 2026.

What is the venue for the summit?

The summit will be held at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC) in Nairobi, Kenya.

Are there any accommodation options available?

Yes, we have partnered with several hotels in Nairobi to offer discounted rates for summit attendees. Please check our website for more details.

Is there a code of conduct for the summit?

Yes. We have a code of conduct that all attendees are expected to follow. Please review it on our website before attending the summit.

Yes, selected sessions will be recorded and made available to registered attendees after the summit.

How can I become a speaker or panelist at the summit?

If you are interested in speaking or participating in a panel discussion, please submit your proposal through our website. We welcome submissions from industry experts and thought leaders.

Are there any networking opportunities at the summit?

Yes, we have dedicated networking breaks, a startup pitch competition, and an exhibition floor where attendees can connect with industry professionals and potential partners.
